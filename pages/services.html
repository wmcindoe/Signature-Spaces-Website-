Our Services

Every service is tailored to your property, your budget, and your vision.

01

Interior Design

Our full interior design service takes you from an empty room — or one that just isn't working — to a beautifully finished space that suits your lifestyle perfectly.

We handle everything: spatial planning, colour schemes, furniture selection, lighting design, artwork curation, and final styling. You're involved at every stage, but we take on all the hard work.

  • Initial consultation & site visit
  • Concept development & mood boards
  • Furniture & material sourcing
  • Supplier & contractor management
  • Installation & final styling
  • Post-installation support

02

Property Staging

First impressions sell properties. Our staging service transforms empty or tired properties into aspirational homes that attract buyers, generate higher offers, and sell faster.

Whether you're selling through an estate agent, on the open market, or at auction, a well-staged property consistently outperforms an unstaged one — often significantly.

  • Pre-sale property assessment
  • Full furniture & accessory styling
  • Artwork & soft furnishing hire
  • Professional photography preparation
  • Occupied & vacant property staging
  • Flexible hire periods
Enquire About This Service

03

Soft Furnishings

The right fabrics and textiles can utterly transform a space. We design and source bespoke curtains, blinds, cushions, bedding, rugs, and upholstery — made to measure and finished to a beautifully high standard.

We work with a handpicked selection of fabric suppliers and skilled makers, ensuring every piece is unique, well-crafted, and a perfect fit for your space.

  • Made-to-measure curtains & blinds
  • Bespoke cushions & upholstery
  • Rug & textile sourcing
  • Bedding & bedroom styling
  • Fabric consultation & sampling
Enquire About This Service

04

Design Consultations

Sometimes you just need an expert eye and a clear direction. Our two-hour design consultation is a practical, focused session where one of our designers visits your home, listens to your challenges, and gives you actionable advice you can implement yourself.

It's perfect if you're in the early stages of a project, have a specific problem to solve, or simply want a second opinion before committing to a bigger investment.

  • Two-hour in-home session
  • Room-by-room assessment
  • Colour & layout recommendations
  • Shopping & sourcing guide
  • Written follow-up notes
